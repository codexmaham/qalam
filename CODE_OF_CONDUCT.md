# Code of Conduct

Qalam is a school notebook. The people who use it, and the people who work on
it, include children and teachers. Be kind, be patient, and assume good faith.

## Expected

- Speak about the work and the script, not about the person.
- Welcome corrections, including corrections about a language you do not write.
- Keep examples appropriate for a classroom.
- Give credit when you use someone else's font, sample, or model.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or sexual comments.
- Handwriting samples or photos of a person shared without their permission.
- Changes that upload a student's work off their computer.

## Reporting

Open a GitHub issue, or email the maintainer listed on the repository, if
someone's behaviour makes the project unsafe. Reports are read by the
maintainer. A person who keeps breaking these rules can be asked to stop
contributing.

This is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
